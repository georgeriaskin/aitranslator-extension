Privacy Policy for TranslateGPT

Effective Date: October 1, 2025

Welcome to TranslateGPT, an AI-powered Chrome extension that helps you translate text instantly on any webpage. We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices. By using TranslateGPT, you agree to these terms.

Introduction

TranslateGPT is developed to provide quick, accurate translations using advanced AI models. We do not sell, share, or misuse your data. Our goal is to enhance your browsing experience while respecting your privacy rights under laws like GDPR and CCPA.

Information We Collect

TranslateGPT collects no personal information from you. Here's what happens:

  • Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save preferences, such as your preferred target language or an optional API key for custom AI services. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Active Tab Access: When you use the extension, it temporarily reads selected text from the currently active webpage (via the "activeTab" permission) to process translations. This access is user-initiated, limited to the open tab, and does not involve storing or transmitting the full page.
  • API Calls: Translations are processed by sending the selected text to our secure server on Vercel (using limited host permissions for https://*.vercel.app/* and specific app URLs). We do not log, store, or retain any translated content or metadata.

No cookies, trackers, or analytics are used. We don't collect IP addresses, device info, or browsing history.

How We Use Your Information

Any data accessed (like text for translation) is used solely to provide the translation service. Local storage improves usability by remembering your settings. We do not use information for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Sharing Your Information

We do not share, sell, or disclose any information about you with third parties. API calls go only to our own servers for processing—no involvement from other services beyond the AI models we integrate (which handle data transiently).

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data during transmission (e.g., HTTPS for API calls). Local storage is secured by your browser. However, no system is 100% secure, so we recommend using strong browser security settings.

Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data, many rights (like deletion requests) don't apply. But you can:

  • Clear local storage via your browser settings at any time.
  • Opt out by disabling/uninstalling the extension.
  • Contact us for questions (see below). For EU users (GDPR), we confirm no data processing occurs. For California users (CCPA), no "sale" of data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We'll notify users via the extension or Chrome Web Store listing. Continued use after changes means acceptance.
